ReSözly Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Version 2, November 1, 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1. This policy describes what data the ReSözly application processes and how it is protected. The operator is Bahrom Saliev (the "Operator", also the "Rightholder").

1.2. This policy takes into account the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data".

1.3. Main principle: the app works without registration. By default, learning data is stored only on the User's device.

1.4. Signing in and cloud backup are optional. If the User signs in with Google or Apple, the app receives the email address (with Apple, possibly a hidden relay address provided by Apple) and the account ID, and a copy of the learning data is stored on Google (Firebase) servers. Without signing in, this data is not collected.

2. What Data Is Processed

Data Where it is stored Why

3. Third-Party Services

3.1. Google Play / App Store process payments, confirm purchases and show the rating and update dialogs. The Rightholder does not receive card numbers or other payment details.

3.2. Firebase (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication for signing in with Google or Apple; Cloud Firestore for storing the cloud backup (servers in the European Union); Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

3.3. Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) on iPhone; Apple may hide the User's real email address.

3.4. Google (Google Sheets, Google Fonts), GitHub and jsDelivr are used to download dictionary updates, images and fonts. While downloading, these services receive the device IP address and technical request data.

3.5. Word pronunciation is done by the device's built-in speech synthesizer according to its settings.

3.6. The app shows no ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

4. Notifications

4.1. The app sends local study reminders only after the User allows it. Reminders are created on the device itself, without sending data to a server.

4.2. Reminders can be turned off in the app settings or in the device's system settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

5.1. Data on the device is kept while the app is installed. Deleting the app removes it from the device. If system backup (Google or iCloud) is enabled, the operating system may keep it in the User's backup; the Rightholder has no access to it.

5.2. The cloud backup and account are kept until the User deletes them. They can be deleted in the app: "Menu" → "Cloud backup" → "⋮" → "Delete account", or by request to the email below. After the account is deleted, the cloud backup is deleted permanently; data on the device stays.

5.3. Crash reports are kept in Firebase Crashlytics for no longer than 90 days.

5.4. A paid subscription status is kept in the App Store and is restored with the "Restore purchases" button. Premium is not part of the cloud backup.

6. Data Transfer

6.1. The Rightholder does not sell, rent or transfer User data to third parties, except for the services described in section 3 and cases directly provided for by the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

6.2. When the User signs in, the cloud backup and account data are stored on Google servers outside the Republic of Uzbekistan. By signing in, the User agrees to this transfer.

7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Users under 13 may use the app with the consent and under the supervision of their parents. Signing in and cloud backup are intended for users aged 13 and over.

8. Security

Data on the device is protected by the operating system. All connections use the secure HTTPS protocol. Only the User can access their cloud backup, after signing in to their account.

9. User Rights

The User may obtain information about the processing of their data, withdraw permission for notifications, sign out, delete the cloud backup and account in the app, and delete data from the device by deleting the app. Questions and requests can be sent to the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

When this policy changes, the app shows the new version with its number and date. If the app starts processing new types of data, the User will be notified before such processing begins.
